# EXP-0022: independent-seed replication of the 10% wiki mixture

**Status (2026-10-24):** complete. EXP-0022 training finished at 1,500 / 1,500 steps; after PC restart, held-out BPB evaluation and the two-seed paired analysis also completed. The final checkpoint is `runs/EXP-0022-25m-wiki-10pct-s2-2026-10-23/checkpoints/step_00001500.pt`; raw scores and bootstrap analysis are in `runs/EXP-0022-25m-wiki-replication-2026-10-23/analysis.json`. Local exploratory study only; source rights/privacy reviews remain unresolved.

## Motivation

EXP-0017 found a 10% wiki/FSC mixture improved wiki BPB while slightly worsening FSC BPB at seed 20261023. The LR grid found 3e-4 preferable to 1.5e-4 for held-out BPB at the tested fixed budget. Before spending more compute on new recipes, this run checks whether the 10% mixture's domain trade-off repeats under an independent matched seed.

Both use the same grown 25M initialization, model, tokenizer, optimizer, 3e-4 peak WSD schedule, precision, batch shape, training duration and seed. The only intended difference is the prebuilt data stream. Primary outcomes are paired document BPB effects on FSC EN/FR and wiki EN/FR. No multiple-choice test scores select the treatment.

The first matched pair (EXP-0014/0017, seed 20261023) and this pair will be reported separately. Two seeds can check directional repeatability, but they are not enough for a precise estimate of between-seed variance or a strong confirmatory claim.

## Results

The seed-2 treatment's final FSC validation loss was 2.17897 versus 2.17697 for its matched FSC-only control, a 0.00200 increase (worse). The paired held-out BPB results show the same small FSC regression and a larger Wikipedia-domain gain:

| Seed-2 matched outcome (wiki-10% minus FSC control) | Mean BPB change | 95% paired document-bootstrap interval | Direction |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| FSC EN validation | +0.00147 | [+0.00120, +0.00180] | Slightly worse |
| FSC FR validation | +0.00080 | [+0.00056, +0.00102] | Slightly worse |
| Wiki EN heldout | -0.13914 | [-0.14964, -0.12998] | Better |
| Wiki FR heldout | -0.10579 | [-0.11676, -0.09656] | Better |

These directions replicate the first matched seed. Across the two seed-specific effect estimates, the mean changes are +0.00142 BPB on FSC EN, +0.00086 on FSC FR, -0.13863 on Wiki EN and -0.10502 on Wiki FR. The document intervals above are conditional on the checkpoints; only two training seeds are available, so no between-seed confidence interval is reported. No multiple-choice accuracy evaluation was part of EXP-0022. This is evidence for a repeatable domain trade-off in these runs, not evidence that the mixture improves general accuracy or overall model quality.

## Limits

This is a two-seed replication pilot, not a broad validation. Both mixture points use the same corpus pipeline; exact 13-gram decontamination does not detect semantic overlap. Source rights and privacy review remain incomplete, so checkpoints and corpus artifacts stay local.
