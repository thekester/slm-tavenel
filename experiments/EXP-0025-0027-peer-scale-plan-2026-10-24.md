# EXP-0025–0027: planned SLM-Tavenel scale comparisons

**Status (2026-10-26):** Same-harness evaluations of SmolLM2-360M and Qwen3-0.6B are complete. EXP-0028's HPLT training and benchmark artifacts are withdrawn from performance interpretation because its token shards used a different tokenizer from the EXP-0024 parent. EXP-0029 has re-encoded HPLT with the parent's pinned tokenizer; its old-corpus control is training and the HPLT 100M arm follows. The 301M growth operator passes the fixed-input function check in float64 (max absolute difference 0.0); the float32 difference is 2.96e-5 from matrix-multiplication rounding after widening. The 512-token, micro-batch-one memory pilot passed at 6.295 GiB peak. EXP-0025's matched 100M/301M HPLT scale comparison is queued after the corrected EXP-0029 checkpoint. MobileLLM-350M and Llama-3.2-1B remain inaccessible behind Hub gating. FSC/HPLT rights and privacy gaps continue to apply; all resulting weights remain local exploratory artifacts.

| Experiment | SLM-Tavenel target | Peer checkpoints | Target config | Status |

These target models preserve the current FSC tokenizer (8,192 tokens), 256-wide residual stream,
80-layer head-wise-gated architecture, and 1,024-token context. They grow the SwiGLU hidden width
from the trained 100M lineage. This keeps tokenization and initialization lineage consistent within
SLM-Tavenel, but yields deeper/narrower architectures than the peer checkpoints. The parameter
counts are 301,074,688 (FFN 4,608), 497,682,688 (FFN 7,808), and 993,134,848 (FFN 15,872).

Each scale should be trained from a function-preserving expansion of its preceding completed
SLM-Tavenel checkpoint. For an architecture-effect claim, add a same-parent continuation control at
the preceding size with the same data order, token exposure, seed and optimizer schedule. Report
token-matched results separately from compute-matched comparisons. One seed per arm is exploratory;
it does not estimate training-seed variance.

The accessible external checkpoints were evaluated at immutable revisions listed in
`configs/eval/peer-models-2026-10.json` using the repository's pinned six-task evaluator. This makes
scoring inputs and harness consistent, not pretraining data, tokenizer, compute budget, context, or
benchmark exposure. The queued public-reference runs cap context at 1,024 tokens to match the
SLM-Tavenel checkpoints; the evaluator records that cap. Model-specific tokenizer behavior and
loading requirements must also be logged.

## Hardware feasibility

The local GPU is an 8,188 MiB RTX 4060 Laptop. With the current FP32-parameter AdamW training path,
weights, gradients, and the two Adam moments need roughly 16 bytes per parameter before activations:
about 4.8 GB at 300M, 8.0 GB at 500M, and 15.9 GB at 1B. Activations, CUDA context, and temporary
buffers are additional. Therefore 500M and 1B cannot train locally with this setup. The 301M model
passed a one-step pilot at sequence length 512 and micro-batch one, with 6.295 GiB peak allocation.
The queued matched arms use 32,768 token positions per update through 64 gradient-accumulation
steps; the shorter sequence is a recorded protocol change from EXP-0024.
