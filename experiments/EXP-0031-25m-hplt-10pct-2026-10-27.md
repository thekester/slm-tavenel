# EXP-0031: 25M continuation with 10% HPLT-rich candidate data

**Status:** complete (2026-10-27 16:40 Europe/Paris). One-seed exploratory follow-up; no confirmatory claim. The HPLT-derived corpus remains unresolved for per-page rights and privacy and is not approved for public weights.

## Motivation and question

EXP-0030 found a domain trade-off: a 100%-candidate continuation improved HPLT development BPB but worsened FSC development BPB, while none of six paired multiple-choice intervals excluded zero. EXP-0017 found that a 10% Wikipedia mixture preserved most of its in-domain BPB gain with a smaller FSC cost. That does not predict HPLT behavior, but motivates a 10% HPLT-rich-candidate dose point.

Does a 90% FSC / 10% HPLT-rich-candidate continuation from the common EXP-0009 parent improve held-out likelihood on HPLT while preserving FSC performance better than the FSC-only continuation in EXP-0030?

## Matched design

| Arm | Initialization | Training stream | Steps | Token positions |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Control | EXP-0009 grown 25M, step 15,000 | FSC scaling | 1,500 | 49,152,000 |
| Treatment | same checkpoint | 90% FSC / 10% HPLT-rich candidate v2 | 1,500 | 49,152,000 |

Both arms use the same 25,723,648-parameter model, tokenizer SHA-256 `558bab3421bc9ee280f2aa9d0c6a82a9bc482e2ecb72a75837e9c3ee7d2dbd39`, seed `20261027`, sequence length, effective batch, optimizer and WSD schedule. EXP-0030's already completed FSC control and its hashed evaluation outputs are reused as the matched control; no additional control training is needed. The HPLT-rich sampling is stratified by English/French and selected by salted document-ID SHA-256 rank. Before ranking, exclude the candidate's retained French-Science-Commons records and any exact text-hash or stable source-ID matches against FSC validation. The final selected document in each language may be truncated to the exact token target and terminated by EOS. The training mixture records source, selection, exclusion, and shard hashes.

Primary outcomes are paired-document BPB intervals on the same FSC validation documents and the same fixed HPLT candidate-v2 development sample used in EXP-0030. HPLT `test` remains sealed. Six pinned multiple-choice evaluations are secondary; all are previously inspected and will not select a confirmatory model.

## Interpretation boundary

This dose was selected after observing EXP-0030 and earlier mixture pilots. The EXP-0030 control and HPLT-rich treatment share candidate-v2 source material with this follow-up. All results remain exploratory and one-seed; document/item bootstrap intervals do not estimate seed variability. If the 10% dose looks promising, the next step is a new-seed replication under a frozen recipe, followed by the sealed HPLT test evaluation. The selected HPLT source set does not establish rights, provenance, or privacy clearance. Keep all EXP-0031 weights local.

## Results

Both arms completed 1,500 steps (49.152M token positions). Lower BPB is better. Differences are treatment minus the already completed, tokenizer-matched EXP-0030 FSC control. Intervals are paired bootstrap intervals over fixed documents (10,000 draws); they do not estimate seed variability.

| Development set | FSC control BPB | 10% HPLT mix BPB | Difference | 95% paired document interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| FSC English | 1.10317 | 1.10445 | +0.00128 | [+0.00104, +0.00152] |
| FSC French | 1.02421 | 1.02485 | +0.00064 | [+0.00041, +0.00086] |
| HPLT English | 1.59057 | 1.50937 | -0.08121 | [-0.08515, -0.07720] |
| HPLT French | 1.36608 | 1.29982 | -0.06626 | [-0.06935, -0.06313] |

At this one seed, the 10% mix substantially reduces HPLT development BPB while adding only a small FSC BPB cost. This is a better observed domain trade-off than EXP-0030's HPLT-dominated mixture. The FSC differences are statistically separated from zero at the document level but small in magnitude; this does not show that the recipe improves every domain.

| Benchmark | FSC control accuracy | 10% mix accuracy | Difference | 95% paired item interval |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ARC-Easy | 0.316 | 0.314 | -0.001 | [-0.013, +0.011] |
| ARC-Challenge | 0.177 | 0.175 | -0.002 | [-0.015, +0.012] |
| HellaSwag | 0.276 | 0.273 | -0.003 | [-0.006, +0.000] |
| Belebele EN | 0.292 | 0.284 | -0.008 | [-0.023, +0.008] |
| Belebele FR | 0.309 | 0.309 | 0.000 | [-0.013, +0.013] |
| WinoGrande XL | 0.511 | 0.518 | +0.007 | [-0.010, +0.024] |

Every paired benchmark interval includes zero, so this experiment does not establish an accuracy improvement. The HPLT development sample was also used in EXP-0030 and the recipe was selected after viewing it. Keep the HPLT `test` split sealed for the fresh-seed replication; do not describe this pilot as confirmatory.
