# EXP-0023: 50M function-preserving growth and FSC continuation

**Status (2026-10-24):** training and evaluation complete. The grown checkpoint contains 49,349,888 parameters. Growth preserved the parent 25M logits exactly on the recorded fixed-input check. All artifacts remain local and exploratory; FSC rights and privacy review remain unresolved.

## Question

At a matched additional FSC token budget, does increasing the grown SLM-Tavenel from about 25M to about 50M parameters improve held-out likelihood and multiple-choice accuracy?

## Matched design

| Arm | Initialization | Data | Seed | Steps | Additional token positions | Status |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Control, EXP-0020 | 25M EXP-0009 checkpoint | FSC only | 20261024 | 1,500 | 49.152M | Complete |
| Treatment, EXP-0023 | Same 25M checkpoint, grown to 50M | FSC only | 20261024 | 1,500 | 49.152M | Complete |

The treatment doubles depth from 20 to 40 layers, preserving width, attention, tokenizer, vocabulary and context length. The repository's block-expansion operator preserved the source model's logits exactly on a fixed input: maximum absolute difference 0 across 256 tokens. Both arms use the same data, token exposure, sequence and batch shape, AdamW, learning-rate schedule, precision and training seed. The experiment tests the effect of adding the extra depth under this continuation budget; it does not compare scratch 50M with scratch 25M or match total FLOPs.

The parent 25M model itself descends from the 10M run, and EXP-0020/EXP-0023 continue it for an additional 49.152M positions. Report inherited and added token exposure separately. Primary outcome is held-out FSC BPB, with paired document bootstrap conditional on these checkpoints. Multiple-choice accuracy on the existing benchmark suite is secondary and exploratory; the items have already been inspected in earlier experiments.

## Results

The 50M run completed 1,500 steps in about 48 minutes, processed 49.152M new token positions, and used a measured peak of 5.24 GB GPU memory. Its final fixed-batch validation loss was 2.17600; the matched EXP-0020 25M control recorded 2.17697.

### Held-out FSC BPB

Lower is better. Positive differences below mean lower BPB for the 50M treatment. Paired bootstrap intervals resample the same held-out documents and are conditional on these two checkpoints.

| Held-out set | 25M control BPB | 50M BPB | Control minus 50M (95% paired document interval) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| FSC EN validation, 685 documents | 1.10295 | 1.10235 | +0.00061 [+0.00058, +0.00064] |
| FSC FR validation, 678 documents | 1.02426 | 1.02374 | +0.00052 [+0.00050, +0.00055] |

The observed likelihood improvement is small but consistent across these two validation splits. The document intervals do not estimate training-seed variation.

### Multiple-choice accuracy

Raw accuracy is treatment minus the matched 25M EXP-0020 control. Differences are percentage points; intervals use paired item bootstrap resampling.

## Limits

This first 50M run is one seed and one initialization lineage. It cannot estimate training-seed variation, establish a general model-size effect, or support a confirmatory accuracy claim. The 25M control has matching added tokens but lower compute. Any artifacts remain local while rights and privacy reviews are unresolved.
