### Interpretation

The different URLs include explicit alternatives such as CC BY-SA 4.0, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, CC BY 4.0 where the corpus label says CC0, publisher default terms, and generic text-and-data-mining policy pages. Some are restrictive alternatives; others do not identify an article license. None can be used to silently replace the license of the exact historical training file. This evidence therefore **does not resolve the affected items**; several records strengthen the case for exclusion from any newly built public-release corpus unless the original source/version and rights are confirmed.

For each record, the retained audit fields include the source ID, source-declared license, current HAL outcome, DOI where available, and Crossref declarations. The source-text hashes do not identify a specific historical HAL file version. One concrete example is `10.1038/s43247-025-02550-4`: Crossref and the TU Delft research portal report CC BY-NC-ND for a published version, while the UPC repository record reports CC BY 4.0 for its deposited copy. The publisher article page identifies the publication; this disagreement across versions/repositories is exactly why the historical HAL file used for training must be identified before rights can be cleared. See [TU Delft](https://research.tudelft.nl/en/publications/benchmarking-shoreline-prediction-models-over-multi-decadal-times/), [UPC repository](https://upcommons.upc.edu/entities/publication/9066a232-0698-41d6-a9bc-41730bf5a7cd/full), and [publisher page](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43247-025-02550-4). Full machine-readable evidence remains in `training_record/hal-file-license-recheck-2026-10-23.json` and `training_record/unresolved-crossref-license-discovery-2026-10-23.json`.

## Remaining privacy work and decision

The broad scan flags **20,808 document IDs** for review, mostly due to discipline labels and sensitive-topic terms. Those signals are high-recall triage, not findings that each document contains personal data. The old training run did not use the later v4 publication-risk filters. The machine scan does not establish absence of names, indirect identifiers, sensitive narratives, or memorized content in learned parameters.

A representative sample and a defined review method could reduce uncertainty, but no sample can truthfully be called exhaustive. A responsible reviewer still must decide exclusions and acceptable residual risk. The review fields remain unsigned. A more defensible route is a new 25M run trained only on a source set screened, recorded, and rights-reviewed before training; the current candidate is not made clean by attaching this addendum.

## Conclusion

The evidence review is now more specific: the 155 HAL cases have been rechecked individually; 15 Crossref alternate declarations are enumerated; and all four residual IP-pattern documents received focused triage without storing article excerpts. The rights issue remains unresolved because the historical source-file version and rights-holder authority are unverified. The privacy issue remains unresolved because broad flagged-content review and model memorization risk are incomplete. Do not mark `source_rights_review_signed`, `manual_privacy_review_signed`, or `public_release_approved` true on this evidence alone.
