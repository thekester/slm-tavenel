# MOT VOA candidate review: 2026-10-21

**Decision:** suitable to consider for a scientifically documented, narrow VOA news-domain
experiment under the corpus's published terms, subject to the project's ordinary privacy, quality,
split, and contamination checks. This review does **not** approve redistribution of trained weights:
that is a separate rights decision because VOA's terms distinguish VOA-produced text from
third-party material.

## Scientific provenance and metadata completeness

For a scientific training run, corpus-level traceability can be reported without reconstructing
every article's byline: identify MOT v1.11, its pinned commit and release, the archive hashes, the
corpus paper, the published CC BY 4.0 collection license, and the source collection (Voice of
America). Preserve stable article IDs/URLs and say explicitly that author metadata is absent for
59.7% of English records and present for the French records in this audit. Do not invent authors or
infer authorship from a missing field. The resulting scientific claim must remain scoped to the
corpus and training setup; it is not evidence of general-domain performance.

This is a transparent metadata limitation, not an automatic reason to discard those articles for
research. A study may use the pinned corpus as supplied and disclose the limitation. If a later
analysis depends on author identity (for example, author-level grouping), use only records where that
field is present or state that the analysis cannot be performed; do not silently impute it.

## Separate rights and privacy checks

VOA's [official English terms](https://learningenglish.voanews.com/p/5374.html) say only text
produced exclusively by VOA is public domain and warn that third-party material on VOA sites is
restricted. VOA's [terms in French](https://www.voaafrique.com/p/5739.html) state the same
exclusive-production condition and restrictions on AP material. VOA Afrique pages can carry AFP or
Reuters bylines.
The MOT collection-level license and paper are relevant corpus-level evidence for research use.
They do not, by themselves, settle whether the project's intended public distribution of model
weights is permitted for every source item. Missing author metadata alone is not evidence of a
third-party rights problem. For any public-weight release, inspect available source labels and
credits, document how the dataset's CC BY 4.0 grant and VOA's third-party-content caveat apply, and
seek a narrower source-level review if the evidence leaves material uncertainty. Clearly separate
that release decision from the scientific validity of a run that cites and describes the corpus.

MOT's documentation does not provide a privacy audit. Run the existing PII/redaction pipeline,
record redaction counts and inspect a stratified sample of kept and removed documents. Keep any
remaining privacy concerns in the model card. CC BY attribution must be recorded; do not assume that
the dataset's CC BY grant resolves rights in third-party material or determines the license for
derived model weights.

## Scientific fit

The corpus is news-domain and has an EN/FR size imbalance. A verified, balanced,
document-disjoint subset may support an experiment about a 1.2M model on VOA news, but not a
general-purpose bilingual SLM claim. Keep source, article, and date metadata through splitting.
Prefer a temporal test window and group syndicated/near-duplicate articles before splitting to
reduce leakage. Recalculate exact train/dev/test tokens after filtering; set the same token budget
for both model arms and all paired seeds. Do not repeat the French subset merely to match the old
491.52M-token run.

## Reproducibility and release checklist

1. Fetch only the pinned English and French v1.11 archives and hash them.
2. Inventory records by article type, original URL, date, title, author/byline, and any source or
   agency label; report missing fields.
3. For analyses requiring author identity, report coverage and restrict that analysis to records
   with usable author metadata. Do not exclude records from general corpus-level research solely
   because the author field is empty.
4. Run quality, exact/near-duplicate, PII, and benchmark-contamination audits on the resulting set.
5. Preserve attribution metadata and document a specific CC BY attribution statement.
6. Record the corpus-level source and license evidence in the source registry. Track public-weight
   redistribution clearance as a separate status, not as a proxy for scientific corpus provenance.

Reproduce the metadata inventory after fetching the two archives with:

```powershell
python scripts/audit_mot_archives.py data/raw/mot-v1.11/eng_voanews.tgz data/raw/mot-v1.11/fra_voaafrique.tgz --output runs/corpus-audits/mot-v1.11-metadata-audit.json
```

The inventory itself is complete for the pinned archives. A prospective research run may proceed
once the project's privacy, quality, contamination, and protocol checks are complete and the
maintainer records the intended use and corpus-level license/attribution. Public model-weight
redistribution remains a separate review because of possible third-party material. The existing FSC
checkpoint was not trained on MOT and cannot be retroactively validated by this source review.
