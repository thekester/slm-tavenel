# Human blind review: Story 935K pilot

This task turns the existing 30 raw development continuations into 15 anonymously labelled comparisons. Each of five public prefixes occurs under three decoding conditions. A/B assignments and case order are randomized locally; the mapping is kept in `runs/EXP-0045/human-blind-pilot-2026-11-10/key.json` and is never served by the form.

No human ratings are generated by an assistant. The evaluator must read English, choose a pseudonym, declare that they are a human and report whether they have consulted the variant identities. Six 0-4 ratings (grammar, coherence and relevance for each continuation) and an A/B/tie preference are required for each pair. Ties count as half a preference in the descriptive summary. An empty, partial or machine-generated file never counts as a completed human blind form.

| Priority | Status | Metric | Evidence | Decision informed | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Existing, transferred to TinyStories | Bits per byte | Frozen model snapshots and official validation texts | Check whether the SimpleStories-domain advantage transfers | Same-byte totals and 256-token chunk policy; paired document bootstrap |
| 2 | Existing rubric, awaiting people | Human preference | A/B/tie form, identities masked | Describe this pilot's reader preference | Ties 0.5; cluster by the five prefixes |
| 3 | Existing rubric, awaiting people | Grammar, coherence, relevance | Six manual 0-4 fields per pair | Explain why a continuation was preferred | Human scores only; no proxy from repetition |

This is an exploratory development pilot. The source outputs and their original model identities have previously been public, so blindness depends on the reviewer not consulting them and disclosing prior exposure. It is not a sealed test, has only five independent prefix clusters and one training seed, and cannot meet the proposed 500-new-prefix confirmatory criterion. Do not present the 15 pairs as 15 independent prompts or one self-declared rater as a population study.

## Run on this PC

```powershell
python scripts/serve_story_review.py --port 8765
# Open http://127.0.0.1:8765 in the browser.
```

The server listens only on loopback. Notes and pseudonyms stay in the ignored `runs/` directory. Other readers can use different pseudonyms on the same PC, or run their own local bundle. Do not share the model key while collecting reviews. No invitations are sent automatically.

After all intended reviewers finish:

```powershell
python scripts/analyze_story_human_review.py
```

The report includes only completed human forms whose blinding declaration is affirmative. It describes per-model trait means and target-model preference, with a paired prefix-cluster bootstrap interval. That interval does not include training-seed or reviewer-population variation. If there are no eligible completed forms, the status is `awaiting_human_ratings`, and quality scores remain null. Raw notes and pseudonyms are not intended for public upload; review aggregate publication before sharing.
