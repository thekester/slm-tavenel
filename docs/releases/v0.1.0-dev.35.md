# v0.1.0-dev.35

## Included

- Publishes revised model cards and aggregate measurement artifacts for all five SLM-Tavenel models on Hugging Face. Exact Hub revisions and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `docs/model-cards/revised-2026-11-06/hf-publication-2026-11-08.json`.
- Adds CPU/GPU inference, KV-cache equivalence, quantization and limited safety-screening measurements to the cards, with limitations stated. No model weights or new inference code were uploaded. Raw generated continuations were excluded from the public files and this Git release.
- Records the H-Net byte-level pilot results in EXP-0040, including its loss against the BPE control and the deviations from its planned compression target.
- Adds a preregistered good-view JEPA experiment on bilingual translation pairs (EXP-0043), parallel-pair preparation, alignment analysis, and training configuration. The v1 treatment collapsed and is documented as a failed pilot; v2 remains an experiment plan/run, not a claimed improvement.
- Adds the EXP-0043 training support code and its unit-test file. Tests were not run for this release.

## Limits

- The model-card check passed for all five cards; this checks card structure and links, not model quality.
- The safety measurements are a basic screening, not a safety certification. Human data-rights and privacy reviews remain pending.
- EXP-0043 has no completed v2 comparison or performance claim in this release.
