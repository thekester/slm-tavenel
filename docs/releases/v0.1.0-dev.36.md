# v0.1.0-dev.36

## Included

- Corrects the Belebele English/French alignment analysis to deduplicate the 900 question items into 488 unique parallel passages; the earlier duplicated-passage output is identified as invalid.
- Records the EXP-0043 good-view v2 result: the preregistered alignment threshold was not met, the BPB guard passed, and limitations are described.
- Adds the preregistered EXP-0046 COCO text-JEPA comparison, data preparation and evaluation scripts, and resumable training queue. Layer selection and primary scoring use separate fixed image halves to avoid selecting and evaluating the best layer on the same images.
- The initial EXP-0046 training attempt was stopped after 150 updates due to memory use; activation checkpointing and chunked NTP loss are documented. No result or model-weight claim is made for this experiment.

## Checks and limits

- Python syntax compilation passed for the three new EXP-0046 scripts. No unit or integration tests were run.
- COCO captions are a small English caption corpus and this experiment cannot reproduce the cited paper's scale or claim that a small model outperforms a 72B model.
- Data-rights and privacy reviews for the broader SLM-Tavenel training corpora remain pending.
