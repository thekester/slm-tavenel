# v0.1.0-dev.19: HPLT corpus growth and 50M/100M research workflows

This GitHub prerelease publishes code, configurations, and research documentation for reproducible corpus preparation and SLM-Tavenel scaling experiments.

## Included

- Pinned Hugging Face dataset screening and a local, filtered HPLT growth-corpus candidate with source metadata, split manifests, hashes, and benchmark decontamination.
- Fixed-split tokenization and matched 50M/100M continuation configurations and records.
- Evaluation and peer-scale planning workflows, plus notes on the limits of candidate datasets and supplementary evaluation ideas.
- Training/evaluation record updates for EXP-0022 through EXP-0028.

## Status and limits

- EXP-0028's 50M and 100M training runs are complete. Its held-out corpus-test BPB and six benchmark comparison are not yet complete; the 50M benchmark scoring job is running locally.
- Benchmark datasets are for evaluation and overlap screening only. They are not training text.
- The HPLT-rich corpus is an exploratory candidate. Page-level rights and human privacy review have not been completed. No model weights, optimizer states, raw corpora, or token shards are included or cleared by this GitHub release.
- All reported scale results use one seed per arm and do not establish training-seed uncertainty or a general scaling law.

## Validation

`git diff --check` completed without whitespace errors. The test suite was not run for this release. The in-progress model evaluation is local and is not included in the release artifacts.
